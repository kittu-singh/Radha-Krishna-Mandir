✦ ABOUT THE TEMPLE

About Our Mandir

Shri Radha Krishna Mandir was established on 22nd March 1992 with the blessings of Lord Radha Krishna and a deep spirit of devotion. Our temple is a sacred place of faith, love, peace, and spirituality. Devotees visit the temple to seek the divine blessings of Shri Radha Krishna through prayers, worship, and devotion. Our mission is to promote Sanatan Dharma, preserve religious traditions, spread spiritual awareness, and inspire love, harmony, unity, and kindness in society. Radhe Radhe! Jai Shri Krishna! .
